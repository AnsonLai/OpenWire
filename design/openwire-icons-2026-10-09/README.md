# OpenWire icon concepts

Six original PNG concepts made with the built-in imagegen tool on 2026-10-09.

Open `index.html` to compare the icons at 172, 48, 32, and 16 pixels, with rounded-square, circular, or square masks. The original files have full-bleed square backgrounds.

1. `01-open-ring.png` — a simplified evolution of the current open ring and wire.
2. `02-wire-w.png` — a continuous wire forming a bold W.
3. `03-editorial-cards.png` — stacked editorial headline cards.
4. `04-signal.png` — concentric arcs with an outgoing news wire.
5. `05-woven-loops.png` — interwoven loops connecting sources.
6. `06-ow-monogram.png` — a compact OpenWire initial monogram.

The complete prompt for each asset is saved in `prompts.json`.

**Selected: 03 — Editorial cards.** The production mark is rebuilt as a clean,
centered vector in `../../icons/icon.svg` and is used by the header, favicon,
regular icons, and padded maskable icons. The original concept PNGs remain here
for reference; production exports are generated with
`../../scripts/generate-icons.mjs`.
